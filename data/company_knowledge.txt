ACME Corp Employee Handbook — 2025 Edition
============================================

1. WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY
-------------------------------
All full-time employees are eligible for remote work up to 3 days per week.
Employees must be available during core hours (10 AM – 4 PM EST) on remote days.
A stable internet connection and a dedicated workspace are required.
Managers may require in-office attendance for team meetings or collaborative sessions.
WFH privileges may be revoked if performance metrics are not met for two consecutive quarters.

2. LEAVE POLICY
---------------
Annual Leave: 20 days per calendar year (prorated for new hires).
Sick Leave: 10 days per year, with a doctor's note required after 3 consecutive days.
Parental Leave: 16 weeks paid for primary caregivers, 6 weeks for secondary caregivers.
Bereavement Leave: 5 days for immediate family, 3 days for extended family.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward (max 5 days) or encashed at year-end.

3. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
-------------------------
Business travel expenses must be pre-approved by the department head.
Meal allowances: up to $50/day for domestic travel, $75/day for international.
Receipts are required for all expenses above $25.
Reimbursement requests must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
Home office equipment allowance: $500 one-time setup, $200/year for maintenance.

4. PERFORMANCE REVIEW PROCESS
------------------------------
Performance reviews are conducted bi-annually (June and December).
The review process includes self-assessment, peer feedback, and manager evaluation.
Ratings: Exceeds Expectations, Meets Expectations, Needs Improvement.
Employees rated "Needs Improvement" will enter a 90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).
Promotions are considered annually during the December review cycle.

5. TECHNOLOGY AND SECURITY
---------------------------
All employees must use company-issued laptops for work purposes.
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is mandatory for all company systems.
Personal devices may not be used to access sensitive company data.
VPN must be active when working outside the office network.
Report any security incidents to the IT Security team within 24 hours.

6. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
-----------------------------
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000.
Approved courses, certifications, and conference attendance are covered.
Employees must share learnings with their team within 2 weeks of completion.
Internal mentorship program pairs junior employees with senior leaders quarterly.
